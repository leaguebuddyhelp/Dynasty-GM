# CUS03 early prospects

`/earlybigboard` includes the CUS03 preseason board. Its ten ranked players and scouting direction come from `bundled_data/madden/Madden - CUS03 - Early Big Board.json`, with authored preseason bios in `early-top-10-bios.json`. These reports do not use later board ranks or final-season statistics.

The ten early-board players and three additional quarterbacks have transparent portraits generated from their appearance details in `bundled_data/madden/Madden - CUS03.json`. `class03-portrait-prompts.json` records the built-in imagegen prompts and image paths. The early board matches portraits by name, position and school, not by rank.

The class 3 source does not yet contain the later scouting notes needed for finished `/prospects` cards. These portraits are stored for that later work and do not publish the players in that browser.
